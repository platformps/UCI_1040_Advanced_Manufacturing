Safety & Energy Isolation (LOTO)

Prioritize safety by executing NFPA 70E lockout/tagout procedures. You will don appropriate PPE, properly isolate the main power disconnect, and use a digital multimeter to perform a strict 3-point Live-Dead-Live verification to guarantee a zero-energy state before opening any control enclosures.
