Phase 3: Theoretical Circuit Analysis

Calculate theoretical parameters for R1 (100Ω) in series with parallel pair R2||R3 (200Ω each) operating on 24.0V DC.

  • Parallel Reduction: Rp = (200 × 200) / (200 + 200) = 100.0 Ω
  • Total Resistance: RT = R1 + Rp = 100 + 100 = 200.0 Ω
  • Line Current: IT = 24.0V / 200.0Ω = 0.120 A (120 mA)
  • KVL & KCL Loops: Verify VR1 = 12V, VRp = 12V, and branch currents I2 = I3 = 60 mA.
Math Rule: Two equal parallel resistors always produce an equivalent resistance equal to half of one resistor ($R/n$).

Phase 4: Benchtop Wiring & DMM Verification

Wire the combination circuit on the SMC BEMATE-200 trainer with banana jumpers and take physical DMM readings within ≤ 5% variance.

  • Unpowered Check: Measure unpowered total resistance RT (195Ω – 205Ω tolerance).
  • Voltage Drops: Measure VR1 and VRp using standard V/Ω lead placement.
  • In-Series Ammeter Mode: Move red lead to the 10A Jack, break circuit branches, and measure in-series line current (IT) and branch currents (I2, I3).
Fuse Blown Hazard: NEVER place DMM probes in parallel across energized loads while set to 10A Current mode!
